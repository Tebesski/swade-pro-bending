# Pro-Benders & Republic City — GM balance reference

Better Setting calculated these ratings from the 106 prepared NPC sheets in Foundry V14. The two mecha use the vehicle rules and have no NPC CR. Each bender started fresh in Balanced Stance, with No Power Points, Unarmored Hero enabled and Wound Cap off. A Wild Card has two personal Bennies here. Extras have none.

## League teams

Team strength is the sum of the three members’ unrounded strengths in Novice-equivalent units. A team at 2.00 has twice the four-round benchmark strength of one reference Novice. The last column gives the lowest and highest win rates against the other three teams in that division. Better Setting ran 2,048 trials per pairing with separate initiative, everyone in reach and victory by incapacitation. Ring-outs, terrain, Support, templates and signature moves can change the result.

## Individual ratings

All 106 ratings have **Partial** coverage. The sheet’s CR models readied attacks and single-target damage. For medics, rescuers and controllers, use the tactics in Notes and check who benefits from their support or loses an action to their control.

The attack column gives a main-hand fallback against a fresh reference PC at the first advance of the NPC’s rank. Anchors use Assault against Parry. Everyone else uses Hurl at Short Range. Hit and Wound are chances per attempt in the open before Soak. A Wound means at least one Wound on an initially unshaken target. Toughness includes the armor in parentheses. The last column gives the first tactic from the sheet.

## Mecha crews

The 4th Battalion pilot drives the Fire support mecha. The 5th Battalion pilot drives the Line breaker. Each crew sheet links its vehicle. Both pilots roll Vehicles d10. Each tank has Toughness 11 (3) and three Wounds. The launcher and grapple arm are mounted weapons. The pilot’s listed CR measures the pilot fighting on foot.
